Latin subsets of two variable fonts, self-hosted through `next/font/local` (see `src/app/layout.tsx`):

- `geist-latin.woff2`: [Geist](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) by Vercel, weights 100-900
- `bricolage-grotesque-latin.woff2`: [Bricolage Grotesque](https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage) by Mathieu Triay, weights 200-800

Both are licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org).
